Understanding the Essence of Artificial Intelligence

Artificial Intelligence refers to the simulation of human intelligence processes by machines, particularly computer systems. These processes include learning (the acquisition of information and rules for using it), reasoning (using rules to reach approximate or definite conclusions), and self-correction. In practical terms, AI can facilitate tasks ranging from simple data analysis to complex decision-making systems.
